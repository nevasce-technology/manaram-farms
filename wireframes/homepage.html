Our path

Twenty-two years of growing.

From a box of dog chews to a national export award — the same family thread, chapter by chapter.

2003 2013 2014 2016 2018 2020 2022
  1. 2003

    Manaram Himalayan Handicraft

    Ram Dai and his sons begin exporting churpi dog chews and handicrafts to the United States.

  2. 2013

    Roots in agriculture

    The family turns toward farming and food processing — laying the groundwork for Manaram Farm.

  3. 2014

    Manaram Farm

    The farm is founded to put Nepal’s local resources to work — milk, grain and spice, processed in-house.

  4. 2016

    Seto Tyami

    The group widens beyond its first export line.

  5. 2018

    Manaram Group

    Eight enterprises are brought under a single umbrella.

  6. 2020

    CIP Award

    The Government of Nepal names Manaram a Commercially Important Person — the country’s highest exporter of agriculture-based products.

  7. 2022

    Momoway

    The group’s food and service arm opens a new chapter.

The farm

We did not want to buy the milk.
We wanted to know it.

Manaram Farm collects milk from across Nepal and processes it under its own roof. Owning the facility is not a matter of pride — it is the only way to hold safety and sanitation to a line we set ourselves, rather than one we inherit from someone else’s factory.

  • 100% Pure cow milk in every Chhurpi pack
  • In-house Processing, start to finish
