# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are people discovering Manaram Farms online: they want to understand who the farm is, what it grows or sells, and why it is worth trusting. They are browsing a public brand site, not an internal ops tool.

## Product Purpose

A public website that tells the Manaram Farms story and showcases products. Success is a visitor who understands the farm, its products, and its character well enough to trust it and want to learn more or buy later.

## Positioning

Manaram Farms as a named farm with its own story and product line, not a generic produce catalog or marketplace.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive through the web to read about the farm and browse products. Product photography and farm/about copy exist with the team; the current codebase is a Vite + React + Tailwind starter without that content checked in.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed: marketing/story site; product showcase; about the farm and products.
- Undecided: e-commerce checkout, bookings, wholesale portal, and account/admin.
- Stack already in the repo: React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Manaram Farms.
- A logo exists and must remain the identity mark. Colors may change around it.
- Product images and supporting copy exist with the team; they are not yet in this repository. Future work must not invent testimonials, customers, prices, awards, or other commercial claims.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo: confirmed by the team; file not in the repo yet.
- Product images and about/product copy: confirmed by the team; files not in the repo yet.
- In-repo: placeholder page copy only (`src/App.tsx`).

## Product Principles

- Story and products share the same site; neither is a sidecar.
- Trust comes from real farm identity, not generic agriculture tropes.
- Do not fabricate proof; use real assets and copy when they are provided.
- Keep the public site scannable: farm, products, about — in that order of understanding.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific standard was set. Default to accessible web basics (readable type, contrast, keyboard focus) until a bar is named.
